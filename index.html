Why work abroad

What an international
career can give you.

Working overseas is a serious decision. Here is what it realistically offers — no inflated promises, just the reasons millions of Indians build careers abroad.

See how the process works
  1. 01

    Earn in stronger currencies

    Gulf and Asia-Pacific salaries are typically several times comparable Indian wages for the same trade — and the saving potential is real.

  2. 02

    Work to global standards

    Modern equipment, safety norms and professional environments sharpen your craft faster than almost any local role can.

  3. 03

    Build a CV that compounds

    International experience signals adaptability and reliability. It raises your value for every role that comes after — abroad or back home.

  4. 04

    Lift your family with you

    Remittances, better education, new options. An overseas job rarely changes just one life — it changes a household's trajectory.

How it works

From Bengaluru
to your boarding gate.

Five clear steps. No confusing jargon, no surprises — you always know what happens next and what it costs in time and paperwork.

All you need to start:
a resume and a valid passport.

  1. 01

    Share your profile

    Send your resume through the form below or on WhatsApp. Tell us your trade, your experience, and where you'd like to go.

  2. 02

    Get an honest assessment

    We review your profile against current employer demand. If your profile isn't ready yet, we tell you exactly what to build first — no false hope.

  3. 03

    Interview with employers

    We shortlist you for verified openings, arrange interviews with the employer, and help you prepare so you walk in confident.

  4. 04

    Offer & documentation

    On selection, we walk you through the offer and contract, then guide your visa documentation and pre-departure formalities step by step.

  5. 05

    Fly — with support after landing

    We stay reachable after you land: joining formalities, early concerns, and questions while you settle into your new role.

candidate.

Ask on WhatsApp
Which countries do you currently recruit for?

Primarily the Gulf — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain — along with Singapore and Malaysia. Live demand changes weekly, so ask us what is open right now for your trade.

How do I apply?

Submit your resume through the enquiry form below, or WhatsApp us at +91 96864 54870 with your name, trade and years of experience. We respond within one business day.

What documents do I need?

To start: a valid passport, an updated resume, and your education or experience certificates. Some roles need trade tests or licences — we tell you exactly what applies to your case before anything moves forward.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the role, employer and destination — anywhere from a few weeks to a few months. We keep you informed at every stage so you are never left guessing.

Do you charge candidates?

Costs vary by role and destination. Whatever applies to your case is explained to you in writing before you commit to anything. A word of caution for anywhere you apply: never hand large sums to an agent who won't put terms in writing.

I have never worked abroad. Can I still apply?

Yes. Many employers hire for skill and attitude, and some train on the job. Share your profile and we will give you an honest assessment of where you stand.

Quick answers

Are you a licensed agency?

Yes. We hold MEA Registration Certificate No. B-3525/UP/PER/1000+/5/11528/2026, issued by the Protector General of Emigrants under the Emigration Act, 1983 and valid until 26 August 2031.

Which countries are live?

The Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain) plus Singapore and Malaysia. Demand shifts weekly — ask for your trade.

What do I need to apply?

A valid passport, an updated resume, and your certificates. Some roles add a trade test or licence — we tell you upfront.

What do you charge?

Never more than ₹30,000 — the legal maximum for registered recruiting agents. Payment is by online transfer or DD only, never cash, and you get a receipt every time.

How long does it take?

A few weeks to a few months, depending on role, employer and destination. You hear from us at every stage.

First time abroad — can I apply?

Yes. Many employers hire on skill and attitude, and some train on the job. Send your profile for an honest read.
